What works: turn steps, priority passing, a LIFO stack, land plays, simple mana costs, casting and two example spell effects. What doesn't: combat, Commander-specific rules, triggered abilities, state-based actions, choice handling and most Magic card text. This is a local test harness, not a live multiplayer game.

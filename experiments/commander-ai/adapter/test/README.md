# Real XMage adapter integration checks

`FourthSeatRealGameTest` builds a four-player `GameCommanderImpl` fixture using real pinned-fork card classes, Commander initialization/rules/watchers, real hands/permanents and mana pools, engine legal-action enumeration, and actual `ComputerPlayerFourthSeat` callbacks. Assertions inspect real stack entries, hand movement, payments, legal combat declarations and selected resolved effects. No fabricated `Action` rankings are passed to the policy.

Run after the isolated adapter build completes:

```sh
adapter/test/run-real-engine-tests.sh /path/to/isolated-built-xmage
```

The optional second argument is an exact runtime classpath **file**, not a Maven-cache directory. The default file is generated by `scripts/build-adapter.sh`. The runner uses the adapter class directory and exact Maven-resolved installed runtime dependencies only (without duplicating reactor classes and jars); it does not glob arbitrary cached jars. It exits 2 if adapter classes or the dependency classpath are absent. It does not start a server, register a player type, deploy, or modify production.

## Coverage

- All ten commanders: actual hand identity/extraction and paid legal casting; unfunded rejection; actual command-zone casting.
- Commander tax: first cast resolves, commander returns to command zone, second cast rejects insufficient mana and pays the additional two mana when funded.
- Krenko: legal/tapped activation; cast goblin-maker first; pass while it is on stack; resolve to three Goblins; activate and resolve to six.
- Lathril: actual ten-other-Elf payment; nine-Elf rejection; preserve a live drain in combat; resolve to all three opponents losing ten life and controller gaining ten.
- Talrand: empty-stack hold; threatening-versus-harmless opposing spell; never counter own spell; reserve UU during own main; cantrip at opponent end step.
- Ghalta: actual power discount with only GG; choose an available lethal opponent.
- Giada: cast commander before follow-up Angel; preserve protection without threat; Banner chooses Angel.
- Goreclaw: develop instead of wasting postcombat Stampede.
- Torbran: amplified Bolt chooses lethal opponent; no phantom bonus with commander absent.
- Aesi: cast an already-funded commander before land; still play land when commander is not payable.
- Gishath: choose a connectable opponent.
- Adeline: another evasive creature attacks while Adeline stays out of lethal blocks.
- Kamahl and Boots: no repeated payment for already-applied effect/attachment.
- Gwenna: actual restricted-mana payment for Kamahl and allocation of both green pips for a real 2GG creature.
- Modal protection: Boros Charm chooses indestructible and survives an actual Wrath; Tyvar’s Stand saves the threatened Elf from actual Swords to Plowshares.
- Combat tricks: Get a Leg Up selects an attacking own Elf rather than an opponent or idle own creature, and is held during upkeep.
- Sacrifice and alternatives: lethal Goblin Grenade sacrifices expendable Goblin rather than Krenko; normally paid Flare of Fortitude preserves valuable creatures through Wrath.
- Variable costs: By Force X=1/2 destroys actual artifacts; Entreat’s XXWWW uses seven/five white for two/one Angels, rejects seven colorless, and handles Sol Ring plus three Plains without double-counting.
- Source-tap payments: Castle Embereth and Myriad Landscape pay from other lands, then actually resolve their pump/search.
- Conditional mana: Giada cannot pay for a non-Angel artifact, but pays for a real Angel; Gwenna remains valid for creature abilities.
- Dependent mana: Selvala preserves her green activation payment before spending output; taxed commander recasts are admitted only when the combined payment is sufficient.
- Further payment boundaries: Myriad with only one other land is not attempted; Flare selects normal payment when funded and expendable white creature sacrifice when unfunded.
- Colour choices: Heraldic Banner chooses White for Adeline and Blue for Talrand.
- Regeneration: Golgari Charm selects regeneration against Murder, but is held against exile.
- Optional costs: Tender Wildguide casts normally with two mana and produces its offspring with four; Smuggler’s Surprise rejects unaffordable Spree and resolves an affordable protective mode.
- Failed payment: a real cast triggers completed engine rollback, restores hand/stack, and clears transient activation/payment context on the current player. This fork restores players in place.
- Costed producers: Nykthos reserves its own tap while paying two input mana, rejects insufficient input, and chooses productive devotion for generic costs. Command Tower plus Forest pays Growth Spiral GU; Simic Growth Chamber remains valid.
- Escalate: Collective Effort pays distinct tap costs for two/three modes, including summoning-sick creatures; admission does not double-commit a required mana dork or one body to multiple costs.
- Variable permanent targets: Curse of the Swine caps X at one/two opposing creatures, pays XUU, exiles them and makes the correct Boars without filling targets with an own creature.
- Optional draw: actual Aesi landfall declines an empty-library draw and still draws when stocked.
- Work avoidance: empty-hand/no-action priority constructs no observation; actual counter and protection scenarios remain covered. Aesi’s large Scute army projection distinguishes ready creatures from tapped or summoning-sick creatures.
- Combat keywords: actual Ghalta/Gishath trample attacks through a lone small blocker but avoids two lethal blockers; Lathril menace attacks past one blocker but respects two.
- Observation: actual Elf/non-Elf/changeling subtypes; opponent hand count without card identity.

`FourthSeatAdditionalCostContractTest` adds 27 checks for typed mode-cost and offspring preparation: exact totals, source immutability, independent copies, no repeated cost accumulation, and no mana-pool mutation.

These are adapter regression checks, **not** full coverage of every card, every rules interaction, or proof of human-equivalent strategic play. Separate real-deck soak tests cover sustained play. The tests intentionally retain failures that expose missing behavior rather than weaken assertions to make the build green.

## Verified validation

On 16 October 2026, the complete suite compiled and ran with the exact Maven-generated runtime classpath: **117 real-engine scenarios passed, 0 failed**, plus **27 additional-cost contract checks passed**, combined process exit 0 (11:22 UTC, following the coherent 11:22:26 adapter build). See `results/latest.txt` and `results/source-sha256.txt` for the corresponding run and source snapshot.

The suite exposed and then verified corrections to Krenko sequencing, Aesi sequencing, Torbran targeting, counter conservation, repeated equipment/animation, Lathril effect classification, keyed creature-type choices, Gwenna multi-colour payment, modal effect/target preservation, sacrifice selection, X announcements, source-tap reservation, conditional and dependent mana, alternative/optional payments, colour choices, regeneration modes and rollback context. A passing fixture suite does not replace the separate full-deck soak report or the owner’s pre-activation review.
